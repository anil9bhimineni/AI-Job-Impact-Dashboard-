## Day 3 - Product manager angle

Will AI replace Product Managers?

My view: not the good ones.

AI will pressure PMs who only write tickets, summarize calls, and pass messages between teams.

But PMs who can define outcomes, evaluate AI products, understand users, and drive business decisions should become more valuable.

## Day 5 - India vs global

AI job risk is not the same in every country.

A role can look risky globally but still grow in India because of:
- digital public infrastructure
- GCC expansion
- fintech adoption
- healthcare formalization
- tier-2/3 city hiring

That is why I made the dashboard country-aware instead of only copying US-style job risk scores.

## Day 8 - Data analyst vs data scientist

Data Analyst vs Data Scientist is becoming an interesting career choice.

Data Analyst:
- broader entry path
- more business-facing
- some reporting tasks may automate

Data Scientist:
- stronger AI tailwind
- higher technical bar
- more model/evaluation work

The safer move depends on your skills, not just the job title.

Compare two careers here:
https://global-ai-jobs-impact-dashboard.netlify.app
