# Roadmap Feature Status

This document distinguishes implemented product behavior from features that require an external provider or authorized institutional setup. “Schema-ready” does not mean a feed or email was delivered.

## Acquisition

- Live: 157 canonical role pages and 2,000+ role × India city/state pages.
- Live: canonical URLs, breadcrumbs, Occupation structured data, sitemap, robots file, and social metadata.
- Live: two-minute task-based AI-safety quiz.
- Live: shareable comparison and score cards with campaign/referral attribution.
- Live foundation: embeddable widget configuration and referral-event schema.
- Live: exposed-role, growth, regional, search, and comparison views in the dashboard.
- Schema-ready: reviewed Hindi and Indian-language content translations. No machine-generated translation is labelled as reviewed.
- Provider-gated: `JobPosting` markup is created only from verified vacancies.

## Retention

- Live: optional magic-link accounts and owner-scoped profiles.
- Live: device saves plus authenticated watchlists and alert preferences.
- Provider-gated: risk, demand, salary, layoff, weekly, and monthly email alerts are hidden until a verified delivery pipeline is operational.
- Live: role-specific 12-week transition plans and skill simulator.
- Live: verified/editorial learning-resource contract, progress/checklist schema, CSV and print/PDF paths.
- Provider-gated: email delivery additionally requires change detection, unsubscribe handling, and delivery monitoring.

## Market utility

- Live: vacancy provider boundary, employer/location/salary/freshness/application contracts, remote/fresher flags, and an honest unavailable state.
- Live: verified vacancies are separate from modelled demand in the schema and UI.
- Live foundation: state/metro locations, salary percentiles, degrees/ITI/diplomas/certifications/exams, and employer hiring/layoff timeline tables.
- Provider-gated: current NCS/job-board and salary records require approved feeds, credentials, and terms.

## Institutional growth

- Live foundation: institutions, role-based membership, cohorts, placement/workforce report queues, API clients, white-label widgets, and score disputes.
- Live: owner-facing career workspace and CSV/print exports.
- Admin-gated: institution creation, membership provisioning, API-key issuance, and dispute resolution.

## Evidence rule

Measured, modelled, and analyst-reviewed values remain separate. Online vacancy postings are never presented as total employment, and missing providers produce a visible unavailable state rather than synthetic data.
